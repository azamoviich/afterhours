FAQ

Why does an app stop opening after a week?

Free Apple IDs sign apps for 7 days. Refresh in SideStore every few days. Your data stays.

"Maximum App ID limit reached"?

Free Apple IDs get 10 App IDs per 7 days, and extensions like Nudge's widget count too. Wait for the week to roll over.

How many apps can I install?

3 at once on a free Apple ID, and SideStore itself is one of them.

Is it safe?

Everything runs on your phone: no accounts, no analytics, no tracking. SideStore re-signs the apps with your own Apple ID.
